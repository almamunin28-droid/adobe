Adobe Tracker Pro — User Guide

Market intelligence tool powered by cloud scraping technology

1. Anti-Bot Cloud Engine

This extractor bypasses Adobe Stock's Cloudflare bot mitigation and retrieves verified marketplace data directly using Apify Proxy.

2. How to connect your Apify API:

  1. Visit Apify Console ➔ Integrations ➔ API Tokens.
  2. Copy your token (format: apify_api_...).
  3. Click the Manage Apify Key button at the top of this page and paste it.
  4. Click Save & Apply.

3. Scoring & Market Opportunity:

  • Opportunity Score (0-100): Measures whether a keyword has high buyer demand relative to market saturation. Calculated via logarithmic download velocity and median benchmarks.
  • AI Saturation: Percentage of top results generated via Generative AI tools (e.g. Midjourney, Adobe Firefly) versus traditional illustrations and photos.
